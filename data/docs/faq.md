# Shipping FAQ

## What are your delivery times?
Standard domestic delivery within Germany takes 2-3 business days. Cross-border
deliveries within the EU typically take 3-5 business days, depending on the
destination country and chosen carrier.

## How do I return a package?
To return a package, log into your account, go to "Order History", select the
order, and click "Start Return". You will receive a prepaid return label by
email within 24 hours. Packages must be sent back within 14 days of delivery.

## What happens if my package is delayed?
If a package is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, our system
automatically flags it for review. Customers can request a status update at
any time, and if a package is delayed by more than 5 business days, customers
are eligible for a shipping cost refund.

## Which carriers do you work with?
We partner with DHL, Hermes, GLS, and DPD depending on the destination and
service level selected at checkout.

## Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Address changes are possible only before the package has been handed to the
carrier. Once an order shows "in_transit", the address can no longer be
changed and customers should contact the carrier directly.

## How do I report a damaged item?
Damaged items should be reported within 48 hours of delivery through the
"Report an Issue" button in order history. Please include photos of the
damage. A replacement or refund will typically be processed within 5 business
days.
